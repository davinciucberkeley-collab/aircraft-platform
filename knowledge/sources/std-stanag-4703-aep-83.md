## Summary
NATO airworthiness requirements for light fixed-wing UAS, with limit and ultimate factors and manoeuvring and gust loads. It applies to aircraft up to 150 kg maximum takeoff weight, with relaxed requirements below 66 J impact energy. It gives no numeric landing load factor in the read portion. The first 100,000 characters were read; later annexes were not.

## Usable rules / numbers
- [landing_load_factor] No numeric landing load factor. Landing impact (UL.6.2) requires the worst combination of reasonable landing loads at maximum design weight; the numbers are in Annex B, not read.
- [landing_load_factor] Limit load factor of safety at least 1.0; ultimate load factor of safety at least 1.5 for hazardous structure and at least 1.25 for other structure (UL2.2, UL2.3).
- [landing_load_factor] Symmetric limit manoeuvring load factor at least 3.8, negative limit at most -1.5 (UL5.2).
- [landing_load_factor] Scope: fixed-wing light UAS up to 150 kg MTOW; impact energy above 66 J is the main scope; no 55 lb limit.

## How we could use it
- Gives a regulatory limit-load anchor (3.8 g manoeuvring) that is higher than 3 g, and a 1.5 ultimate factor. Does not confirm a 3 g landing factor.
- Consider a manoeuvre-based load case as an alternative to a single landing factor.
