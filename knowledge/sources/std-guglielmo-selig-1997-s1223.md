## Summary
Wind-tunnel study of the S1223 high-lift airfoil at low Reynolds numbers, with comparison airfoils. It tabulates maximum lift coefficients at Re of about 2e5 to 2.5e5 for ten airfoils. Values range from about 1.17 for a low-lift section to 2.2 for the S1223.

## Usable rules / numbers
- [cl_max_default] Clean S1223 CLmax about 2.2 at Re 2e5 (abstract; section V; Table 1). With vortex generators or a 1 percent Gurney flap, 2.3 (section V; Figs. 16 and 17).
- [cl_max_default] Comparison airfoils at Re 2e5 (Table 1): E423 2.00; FX 63-137 1.75; M06-13-128 1.52; E214 1.25; S3021 1.17. LNV109A 1.87 and LA2573A 1.86 at Re 2.5e5.
- [cl_max_default] FX 63-137 given as 1.7 to 1.8 for Re 2.5e5 to 7e5 (section III), which only partly overlaps the default's Re range.
- [cl_max_default] Only the S1223 is described as high-lift; the paper's own comparison sets lower values for conventional low-Re sections.

## How we could use it
- Gives the most direct CLmax numbers at small-UAV Reynolds numbers found in this scout. Lowest plain airfoil is 1.17, so a 1.1 default is below all sections tabulated here.
- Use as a Tier 2 check for stall speed on unknown airfoils, not as a typical value.
