## What's novel
A single-motor electric ultralight with no ailerons. Roll stability comes from high wing dihedral and the rudder is on the pedals. The motor was borrowed from the OpenPPG project. The wings were lengthened to add lift.

## How it was built
- Foam sections are hot-wire cut from styrofoam, and the wings carry aluminum tube spars.
- One large brushless motor, borrowed from the OpenPPG project, drives the aircraft.
- The article does not give a build time or total cost.

## Problems and fixes
- A two-blade propeller needed full throttle to take off, which the article compares to driving in first gear. The builder moved to three blades with higher pitch.
- Tail weight is an improvised water bottle taped to the tail. Commenters called this unsafe.
- Commenters raised concerns about stall margin and structural adequacy. The article does not settle them.

## Lessons for us
- Hot-wire foam with a tube spar is a cheap, fast wing build, but the ballast and stall questions need proper engineering before anyone copies the design.
- A single high-power motor cuts parts count, but the propeller choice changed the takeoff behavior a lot.
