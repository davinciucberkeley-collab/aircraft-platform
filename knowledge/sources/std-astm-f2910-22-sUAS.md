## Summary
ASTM standard for design and construction of small UAS. It requires structures to carry landing impact loads without damage, but gives no number for those loads. It sets an ultimate requirement of 1.5 times limit loads. The URL is a third-party sample copy of F2910-22.

## Usable rules / numbers
- [landing_load_factor] Section 5.2.3 (p. 3): the sUA must withstand normal landing impact loads without damage that affects later flights. No magnitude given.
- [landing_load_factor] Section 5.6.10.1 (p. 4): conventional landing gear must accommodate normal landing impact loads without structural damage. No magnitude given.
- [landing_load_factor] Section 5.2.1.1 (p. 3): the structure must not fail at 1.5 times the limit loads. This is an ultimate factor, not a landing factor.
- [landing_load_factor] Section 5.9 (p. 4): launch and recovery loads are deferred to an unnamed consensus standard. No value.
- [hover_thrust_to_weight_min] None.

## How we could use it
- Confirms that a sUAS design standard requires landing loads but does not set their size. The 3.0 value still has no source in this standard.
- Cross-check: the 1.5 ultimate factor matches ASTM F3298 and STANAG 4703 (see existing notes).
