## Summary
US regulation for landing and ground load conditions for Part 23 (normal, utility, acrobatic and commuter category) airplanes. It sets a sink speed range and minimum limit load factors for the landing condition. It applies to manned airplanes. The official eCFR page refused automated access (robots.txt), so this text comes from a public mirror of the regulation.

## Usable rules / numbers
- [landing_load_factor] Paragraph (g): the inertia load factor used for design may not be below 2.67, unless rough-terrain taxi at up to takeoff speed would exceed it.
- [landing_load_factor] Paragraph (g): the limit ground reaction load factor at design maximum weight may not be below 2.0, with the same taxi exception.
- [landing_load_factor] Paragraph (d): descent velocity is given by V = 4.4 (W/S)^(1/4) in ft/s, bounded to between 7 and 10 ft/s.
- [landing_load_factor] Applicability: manned Part 23 airplanes. The paragraph does not mention UAS, so this is a reference floor, not a UAS rule.

## How we could use it
- Gives the lowest limit landing-type load factor found in a published regulation: 2.67 for inertia load. This is below our current 3.0.
- Use as a regulatory floor only. Its applicability to small UAS (under 55 lb) is not established.
