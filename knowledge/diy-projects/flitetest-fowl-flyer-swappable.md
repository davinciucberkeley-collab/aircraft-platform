## What's novel
A smaller version of the original Fowl Flyer cargo airplane that uses a swappable power pod, so the motor and electronics can move between airframes. The fuselage folds from one sheet and self-squares during assembly.

## How it was built
- Foam board is the only material, and the article says it can be made from a few dollars of board. It gives no total.
- The fuselage uses a folded "B-style" fold that self-squares during assembly.
- The nose has an access door, which makes the power pod easy to swap.
- A camera mount is included for FPV flying.

## Problems and fixes
none reported. The only tip is from a reader: swapping two motor wires runs the prop backwards for a pusher setup.

## Lessons for us
- A swappable power pod on a foam fuselage lets one motor and ESC serve more than one airframe, and a reader tip shows that direction can be reversed by rewiring.
- The article gives no wingspan, weight or flight result, so it cannot be used for sizing without measuring a built copy.
