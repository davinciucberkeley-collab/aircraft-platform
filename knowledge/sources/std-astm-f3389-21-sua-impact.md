## Summary
Test method for judging how safe a small UAS (under 55 lbf) is when it impacts the ground near people. It sets drop-test impact speeds for injury assessment, not landing loads. It has no landing load factor.

## Usable rules / numbers
- [landing_load_factor] Section 1.1 (p. 1): scope is small UAS under 55 lbf in the US, for impact safety around people. Not a structural load rule.
- [landing_load_factor] Section 1.6 (p. 1): mentions "load factor" only in general terms when comparing impact force with head-injury levels. No number.
- [landing_load_factor] Sections 12.1.3.4(3) and 12.1.4.3 (p. 7): drop-test impact speeds of 8 ft/s and 16 ft/s for parachute-equipped sUA. These are injury test conditions, not landing sink rates or load factors.
- [hover_thrust_to_weight_min] None.

## How we could use it
- Not usable for the landing load factor. Do not convert the 8 and 16 ft/s speeds into a g value without a stated method.
