## Summary
Blog post that questions the common static-margin rule of thumb and argues that handling qualities depend more on dynamic margin. It names a 5 percent static margin as a widely used heuristic and says it works for many airplanes. It gives no numeric upper bound and no dynamic margin values.

## Usable rules / numbers
- [static_margin] A 5 percent static margin is described as a rule of thumb followed by most aircraft designers, and as reasonable for many airplanes (main text, post body).
- [static_margin] The post argues static margin alone is not sufficient and recommends designing to a dynamic margin instead. No numeric dynamic-margin values are given.
- [static_margin] No range or upper limit is stated; the 15 percent figure is not in this source.

## How we could use it
- Supports the 5 percent lower bound as a heuristic. Does not support the 15 percent upper bound.
- Suggests a future check on dynamic stability, not just static margin.
