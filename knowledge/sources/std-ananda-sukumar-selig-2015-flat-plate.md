## Summary
Study of low-Reynolds-number flat-plate finite wings at Re of about 6e4 to 1.6e5. It defines CLmax by a moment-curve criterion rather than the usual lift-curve peak. The paper does not test named airfoils.

## Usable rules / numbers
- [cl_max_default] Flat-plate wings at Re 6e4 to 1.6e5 give CLmax between 0.55 and 0.7 (section 3.2 and conclusions, section 4). Note this is a finite-wing value with a non-standard CLmax definition.
- [cl_max_default] CLmax rises slightly with Reynolds number and changes little with aspect ratio (section 3.2, figures 14 and 15).
- [cl_max_default] The paper does not give a CLmax for the named FX 63-137 airfoil, which appears only in sting interference tests.

## How we could use it
- Shows that bare flat-plate wings are far below 1.1, so a 1.1 default assumes a cambered airfoil with flaps or a finite-wing lift curve from a real section. It does not set a typical airfoil value.
- Warn that CLmax definitions differ across sources before comparing numbers.
