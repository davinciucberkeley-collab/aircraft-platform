## What's novel
A flying wing cast in a foam-and-MDF mold, with floor underlay as the outer skin and expanding foam filling the core. The skin doubles as a finish, giving a silver look without paint.

## How it was built
- MDF and foam form the mold.
- Floor underlay is laid into the mold as the outer skin.
- The mold is filled with expanding foam and left to cure.
- Cured parts are assembled into the wing.

## Problems and fixes
- Commenters raise fragility and handling concerns; the article does not answer them.
- A release agent that fails can leave expanding foam sticky and never hardening; one commenter suggests Vaseline as the release agent.

## Lessons for us
- Mold release is a known failure point for expanding foam; test it on a small part first.
- The article reports a successful first flight but no measured data, so treat it as qualitative evidence.
