## What's novel
A scratch-built foamboard fighter inspired by a Disney "Planes" racer and modified from the FT Spitfire. It uses a swappable power pod, and the article notes that racer variants are possible.

## How it was built
- Plans and a build tutorial are offered free. The page links to them, but the URLs are not in the text.
- Foam board is the only structural material, and the canopy and scoops are simple cut shapes.
- The finish is Minwax.
- The design has no landing gear, because the designers rarely fly where it would be needed.

## Problems and fixes
- A reader found that the plan's 4.5 mm foam thickness did not match the 5.5 mm board sold in Europe, which could affect fit and weight. The reader asked for DXF files.
- A reader suggested block EPO or EPP foam for the canopy and scoops to simplify building.

## Lessons for us
- Foam thickness varies by region, so check board thickness against the plan before cutting.
- Without landing gear the plane relies on a smooth belly. Plan the launch method before the build.
