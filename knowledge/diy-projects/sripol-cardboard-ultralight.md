## What's novel
A cardboard ultralight, described as the builder's fifth airplane project. The wing spar is cardboard with horizontal flutes, and the main load path is sized by testing sand-loaded beams. The article gives wings of about 44 lb and a whole aircraft of about 120 lb empty (about 54.4 kg, converted for mass_g), with 150 lb once the power system is added.

## How it was built
- The spar is made from cardboard with flutes running across it, and the wing is covered in brown craft paper.
- Sand-loaded beams were tested before the full structure was built, to check the spar.
- The article says the spar carries the wing loads over a 13.5 ft span.

## Problems and fixes
- A full cardboard skin was tried and dropped because it added too much weight.

## Lessons for us
- Load-testing a spar on a beam first is cheap, and it found the weight problem before the aircraft was finished.
- The article shows no flight. Treat the mass and structure figures as build-stage numbers only.
