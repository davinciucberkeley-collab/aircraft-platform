## What's novel
A scale V-22 Osprey RC model in which the wing tilts on wingtip mounts. The revised tilt drive uses two servos, one recessed in each wingtip mount, rather than one servo at the wing root.

## How it was built
- The wing combines 3D-printed ribs, carbon fiber, balsa and the tilt mechanics.
- Shafts run through bearings in the ribs, with a printed centre block connecting the wing to the drive servo.
- An early foamboard profile was built for hover tests.

## Problems and fixes
- The first single-servo design had too much slop in its torque tube, especially at the U-joint and translation horn.
- Fixed by moving to a dual-servo setup with a tighter wingtip mount, tensioned gears and a mount that could be cut free and reglued.
- The author called the single-servo design fundamentally too loose and expected it to make flight unstable.

## Lessons for us
- Measure backlash in any tilt drive before building the wing around it.
- Mounting each tilt servo at its own wingtip avoids a long torque tube and its joint play.
