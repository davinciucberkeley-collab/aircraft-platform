## What's novel
A one-seat, twin-motor electric biplane built mostly from extruded foam board and laser-cut parts. The builder put the cost at "probably around $6,000," which is low for a crewed ultralight. The article gives an empty weight of about 210 lb (about 95 kg) plus about 30 lb of batteries, so mass_g is that empty figure converted.

## How it was built
- Most of the airframe is extruded polystyrene foam board, with laser-cut parts cut to shape.
- The article says aviation-grade hardware is used in the build.
- Two electric model motors of 150 cc each give about 13 hp in total.
- The article says the build took about two and a half months.

## Problems and fixes
- An early, unintended first flight ended in a cornfield and left a dent on the underside.
- After repairs and fixes the aircraft made its maiden flight in October and flew about 20 minutes. It was trailered to the show.

## Lessons for us
- Laser-cut foam parts plus model-class motors keep a crewed-scale ultralight near a few thousand dollars, but the cost is the builder's own estimate.
- The first flight was not controlled. Plan a test regime and an accident-repair budget before the first flight.
