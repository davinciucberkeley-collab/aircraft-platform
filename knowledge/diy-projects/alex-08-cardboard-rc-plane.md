## What's novel
A full RC airplane built mostly from cardboard cut from one large box, with ordinary RC electronics and 3D-printed fittings. The headline says it flies. The article itself gives no flight details.

## How it was built
- The main structure is cardboard from a single large box.
- Off-the-shelf RC parts are used, with 3D-printed components added.
- A commenter points to a detailed Instructables guide and says it includes STL files. We did not open that guide, so treat this as unverified.

## Problems and fixes
- None described in the article.

## Lessons for us
- Cardboard plus 3D-printed fittings is a low-cost route, but the article gives no mass or cost, so it is not yet comparable to foam builds.
- Check the Instructables guide itself before relying on its build steps.
