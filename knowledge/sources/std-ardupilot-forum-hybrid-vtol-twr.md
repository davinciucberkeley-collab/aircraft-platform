## Summary
Community forum thread on the thrust-to-weight ratio for hybrid VTOL aircraft. It contains opinions from individual builders, not an official rule. One poster quotes a published range of 1.2 to 1.5 and a stated minimum of 1.05; another suggests about 2 to 2.2 for lift motors.

## Usable rules / numbers
- [hover_thrust_to_weight_min] Poster "Feibernaut" (Jan 2022) quotes a paper that most VTOL aircraft aim for 1.2 to 1.5, and says the paper's underlying source sets a floor of 1.05. The 1.2 to 1.5 range is his own practice (opinion; first post in thread).
- [hover_thrust_to_weight_min] Poster "0x48 / Daniel Craig" (Oct 2024) says a 2 to 2.2 ratio is suitable for most quadplane frame sizes for lift motors (opinion, introduced with "I think").
- [hover_thrust_to_weight_min] Poster "Ben_Stanton" (Jan 2022) cites the 1.2 to 1.5 range but asks whether it applies only to tail-sitters. No value stated (question).

## How we could use it
- Suggests 1.5 is inside the community range but at the low end; 2.0 is common in practice. Not a rule.
- Need the underlying paper (the one quoted for 1.2 to 1.5 and 1.05) to firm up the value.
