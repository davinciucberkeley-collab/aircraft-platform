## What's novel
A shuttle-shaped foam glider, dropped from an RC airplane in this episode, with model rocket motors attached to its boosters. This is Part 2 of a four-part series from Project Air, and the page is a list of the videos with links to plans.

## How it was built
- The airframe is foam board, with parts improvised from the free online plans.
- Estes solid rocket motors are attached to the boosters.
- The page links a store page for the plans (projectair.co.uk/store), but the store could not be opened.

## Problems and fixes
- The page gives no problems or fixes. A later episode in the same series is listed as a difficult launch attempt, which this page does not describe.

## Lessons for us
- A foam-board glider with free plans is a cheap way to test glide behavior before adding rocket power.
- Drop tests from a carrier aircraft are a useful first step for a glider that will later be boosted.
