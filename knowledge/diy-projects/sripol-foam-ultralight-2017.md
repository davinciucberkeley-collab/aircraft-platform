## What's novel
A home-built electric ultralight made mostly from foam board. The builder started it after attending AirVenture 2017. The article gives a 22 ft wingspan and 17 ft length.

## How it was built
- Foam board insulation makes up most of the airframe, with fiberglass, wood and some aluminum added for strength.
- Battery packs power model-airplane motors instead of a combustion engine.
- The build was filmed in a video series starting August 2017 and reached a mostly complete airframe by late October 2017.

## Problems and fixes
- None reported in the article beyond a short takeoff run. The article does not describe damage or repairs.

## Lessons for us
- Foam board plus fiberglass and wood gives a large, low-cost airframe, but the article gives no cost or mass, so we cannot compare it on price.
- The article does not name the design or say how many motors it uses. Check the builder's own video notes before using it as a reference.
