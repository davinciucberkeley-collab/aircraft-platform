## What's novel
A foamboard VTOL whose tilting section is driven by servos, with motors mounted on a dowel and on the back section. The article is a mid-build log that ends with a crash during the first hover.

## How it was built
- The fuselage is foamboard, stiffened with stiff sign board glued on both sides.
- Hot glue joins the parts throughout.
- A carbon fibre tube and a dowel, shortened with a rotary tool, support the back section.
- Instamorph stiffens the servo mounts.

## Problems and fixes
- During a hover the back motor cut out and the aircraft crashed.
- The cause was still under investigation at the time of the article.
- Stiffening the fuselage raised the weight, which the author noticed afterwards.
- No fix is reported, because the wings were not yet attached and no flight after the crash is shown.

## Lessons for us
- A single motor cut during hover ended the test, so a VTOL foam build needs a power or motor-loss check before the first hover.
- Stiffening foamboard with glue and board adds weight quickly, so track mass as the build goes.
