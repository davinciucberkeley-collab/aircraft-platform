## Summary
NASA technical standard giving structural design, test and service-life factors for spaceflight hardware. It is not a mandatory standard and does not mention aircraft or UAVs. It is not applicable to small UAV structures.

## Usable rules / numbers
- [landing_load_factor] None for small UAVs. The standard covers spaceflight hardware only; no aircraft or landing load factors on the page.
- [static_margin] None.
- [cl_max_default] None.

## How we could use it
- Does not apply to small UAVs. Do not use it to justify any structural factor in design-standards.yaml.
