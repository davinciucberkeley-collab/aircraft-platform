## What's novel
A 3D-printed RC aircraft printed in a lightweight PLA (LW-PLA) with 25 printed parts, held together by adhesive and a carbon tube. The post is a filament-maker guide, so claims about handling are unverified.

## How it was built
- Printed in LW-PLA at 0.2 mm layers, 200 to 230 C nozzle, 20 to 40% infill on structural parts, less on wings and non-load-bearing parts.
- Print volume needed: 220 x 220 x 250 mm; a PDF print profile is offered, but its values are not on the page.
- Joined with UHU Alplast universal adhesive.
- A carbon tube reinforces the fuselage and wings; screws mount the engine.

## Problems and fixes
none reported

## Lessons for us
- The post gives a per-part print recipe with no total print time or cost, so cost remains unknown.
- Performance statements come from the filament seller, so use the 520 g mass as the only measured-looking figure, and treat it as unverified.
