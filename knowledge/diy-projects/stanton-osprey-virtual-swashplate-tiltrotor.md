## What's novel
A scale-style V-22 Osprey RC model in which each rotor uses a "virtual swashplate" built from angled hinges and fast changes in motor speed, instead of a mechanical swashplate and servos. Each rotor carries a third blade to resemble the full-scale aircraft.

## How it was built
- Rotor blade pitch is set by 3D-printed hinges and motor angular-momentum changes rather than servo-driven swashplates.
- The article gives no details on the tilt mechanism, wing, or airframe construction.

## Problems and fixes
- Airframe resonance appeared during testing. The article says the builder overcame it.
- Adding a third blade could upset balance. The article says the technique still worked.
- The article says this is part one of a series and that the control mechanism is expected to change.

## Lessons for us
- A virtual swashplate can give collective and cyclic control without a swashplate, which may simplify tilt-rotor rotor hubs.
- The article treats the build as in progress; check the follow-up episodes before relying on it.
