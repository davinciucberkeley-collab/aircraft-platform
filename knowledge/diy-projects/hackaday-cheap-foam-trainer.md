## What's novel
A foamboard trainer whose wing is shaped by cutting with a heated guitar string, so the only specialist tool is a cheap heated wire. The builder's own nickname is a "$5 trainer," but the article says real cost will be higher without spare parts on hand.

## How it was built
- Foamboard makes up the wing, fuselage, and tail surfaces; the article suggests reusing old packing foam.
- A heated guitar string cuts the foamboard. The wing airfoil is the step the article calls most important.
- Fuselage and tail are described as simpler cuts.
- Landing gear is optional.

## Problems and fixes
none reported (article covers the build, not a flight; it notes low cost makes crash repairs easy)

## Lessons for us
- A heated string is the only cutting tool the article requires for the main shapes, which keeps the tooling cost low.
- The article gives no exact total, so any cost comparison from it needs a separate parts list.
